    How slices work

    Four steps, one owner.

    1. 1

      Choose

      Pick one lesson from your gbrain.

    2. 2

      Approve

      A human reviews it before sale.

    3. 3

      Seal

      Signed and hash-pinned. Revocable.

    4. 4

      Release

      Only after a human-signed, finalized transaction.
