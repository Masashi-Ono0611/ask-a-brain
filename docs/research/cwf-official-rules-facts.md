# Colosseum Crypto World's Fair — facts from the OFFICIAL RULES (verified 2026-10-08)

Source: "Crypto World's Fair Hackathon Rules" PDF, https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf (text extracted locally from the PDF; read sections 3, 5, 8).

- §5 Timing: the Contest Period starts 6:00am PT on September 14, 2026 and ends 11:59pm PT on October 12, 2026 (= 2026-10-13 15:59 JST). Winners announced by December 5, 2026. Registration on the colosseum.com platform is also required before 11:59pm PT on October 12, 2026.
- §8 Judging criteria (six, in this order):
  (a) Functionality — how well it works; code quality.
  (b) Potential Impact — size of the total addressable market; impact on the broader crypto ecosystem.
  (c) Novelty — how unique the concept is.
  (d) UX — how well it uses blockchain to create great UX for downstream users.
  (e) Open-source — is the submission open-source; how well does it compose with other primitives in the crypto ecosystem.
  (f) Business Plan — is there a viable business to build around it; how adept is the team at executing.
- Consequences for this project: the repository must be public with an OSS licence at submission; a custom (non-x402) payment rail must be explained against the "composes with other primitives" criterion; the "startup-style 7 criteria" used earlier in our briefs came from a different Colosseum page and are NOT the official list.
- Not found in the extracted rules text: a clause on pre-existing code or AI-generated code. The earlier statement "pre-existing code allowed if disclosed, judged only on work in the window" comes from colosseum.com/hackathon (a web page) and should be re-checked on the submission form.
- Internal packaging target stays earlier than the official end (suggested: submit by 2026-10-12 18:00 JST), to leave margin for platform registration issues.
